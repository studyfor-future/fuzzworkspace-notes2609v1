Summarize:
At OpENer `v2.3-558-g1e99582` (commit `1e99582`), the report documents an out-of-bounds read in the CPF parser's `CreateCommonPacketFormatStructure()` (`source/src/enet_encap/cpf.c`). The report attributes it to trusting the CPF `item_count` taken from message data without consistently validating later reads against the supplied `data_length`. In the documented debug case, a 16-byte CPF slice beginning `01 e8` was interpreted as `item_count = 0xe801`, and parsing continued after `data` reached `cpf_start + data_length`. The observed result is an out-of-bounds read reported by ASan as `AddressSanitizer: heap-buffer-overflow` in `CreateCommonPacketFormatStructure`; in a non-ASan debug session, the first invalid read was observed around `source/src/enet_encap/cpf.c:280`.
