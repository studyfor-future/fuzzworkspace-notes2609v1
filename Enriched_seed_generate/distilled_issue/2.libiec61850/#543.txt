Summarize:
libiec61850 v1.5.1 is reported to have a NULL pointer dereference in sem_wait when a server receives a specific message. The report attributes this to the semaphore pointer being NULL or not properly initialized before use. The demonstrated result is a Segmentation Fault (SEGV).
